The trees grow thicker here, branches interweaving to block the pale sunlight. The mist swirls around your feet like living tendrils.  
You hear distant whispers — or is it the wind? Every step forward feels heavier, as if the forest is testing your resolve.

Ahead, the forest opens to a small clearing. Shadows shift unnaturally, and a faint amber glow flickers among the roots.
